Captured!

EXT. UNDERWORLD, PIRATE SHIP - CONTINUOUS

NARRATOR

Peeking over the gunwale, Benjamin was unsurprised to find that the dead pirate music was coming from… dead pirates. But unlike most of the dead, these pirates were not entirely… human-looking. Some had teeth that resembled fangs. Others had eyes that seemed to glow yellow. Like being dead had made their outsides match their rotten insides.

Suddenly, Benjamin felt something claw-like twist up the back of his shirt and jacket and suddenly he was being lifted straight into the air. Looking down, he met the gaze of a gaunt, grinning, greenish, golden-eyed grandee, who could only have been… the captain.

CAPTAIN ROBERTS

A round of applause, gentlemen, for our agile young alpinist — the rope-running rapscallion — the peerless, pocket-sized paladin — the plucky, palpitating, prodigiously…

(a beat as he flails, then with a flourish)

…preposterously pertinacious pilgrim of the pellucid provinces!

Silence on deck. Then, from near the mainmast: the rustle of old pages turning. A bony finger dragging down a column. A skeletal jaw clicking softly as it tests the words. A long pause.

SKELETON LIBRARIAN

(flat, bookish, unhurried)

Yep. Checks out.

The deck ERUPTS. Bone mugs slam the rails. The accordion staggers back to life. The fiddle saws into a jig. The dead cheer — a dry, rattling sound, like wind through a boneyard.

NARRATOR

The Captain, for that’s who he was, put Benjamin down on the deck, swept off his cracked, leather hat and bowed low — once to port, once to starboard and finally to Benjamin.

CAPTAIN ROBERTS

My name is Captain Bartholomew Roberts and this is my ship, the Naglfar. And this assemblage of scurrilous scalawags is unfortunately my crew. We were placin’ bets on whether you’d make it to the ship or get swallowed by a shadowsucker along the way.

(to pirates)

Now, settle up, you rancid ratbags!

Mixed reactions as the pirates do so.

CAPTAIN ROBERTS

Now, what might your name be, o utterly unexpected one?

BENJAMIN

Benjamin.

CAPTAIN ROBERTS

Benjamin? There must be something more to it. Benjamin Johnson? Benjamin Franklin? It can’t be just “Benjamin.”

BENJAMIN

Benjamin the Sorcerer. I come from the world of the living.

CAPTAIN ROBERTS

Did you hear that, boys? We got a living boy in our midst and a sorcerer, no less! And why, o whelpish wizard from the waking world, have you graced our dirt-drizzled decks this day? Are you lost? Looking for passage? Or are you just stupid?

BENJAMIN

I have come find my parents.

CAPTAIN ROBERTS

Have you, now? Well, how did they die?

BENJAMIN

They didn’t. They were kidnapped.

CAPTAIN ROBERTS

Well, we don’t have them, if that’s what you’re thinking.

BENJAMIN

I don’t know where they are. Which is why I need the seeing stone.

CAPTAIN ROBERTS

Seeing stone?

BENJAMIN

The one you stole from the demon of the deep.

A MURMUR runs through the crew, like “ooooooh, them’s fightin’ words.”

CAPTAIN ROBERTS

Stole? That’s an ugly word. I prefer claimed. I prefer took. The stone was sitting in a temple with no one watching it. Finders keepers, boy. That’s the law of the sea — and the law beneath it.

BENJAMIN

Well, what do you want for it?

CAPTAIN ROBERTS

What do you have?

BENJAMIN

Gold. Money.

CAPTAIN ROBERTS

Is it in your pockets, like?

BENJAMIN

I can get it.

CAPTAIN ROBERTS

With your magic, maybe?

BENJAMIN

It doesn’t work like that.

Roberts chuckles evilly.

CAPTAIN ROBERTS

So you’re rich, then? Is that it?

BENJAMIN

How about I give it back? Once I know where my parents are, I won’t need it anymore.

CAPTAIN ROBERTS

I admire your courage. Or your stupidity. Hard to tell the difference at your age. So let me give you some advice, boy, for free.

Go home. Find your little boat and row back to whatever shore you came from. By now, your parents are dead. Do you understand what that means? It means they’re done. Finished. They belong here now. What exactly do you think you’re going to do, drag them back to the land of the living? Put them in their old beds and pretend nothing happened?

BENJAMIN

(angry)

I’m not leaving this ship ’til I get that stone.

Word Duel

CAPTAIN ROBERTS

(low, menacing)

Is that so, you artless, arrogant, aggravating aberration?

BENJAMIN

Indeed, it is, you… you selfish, sniveling, spineless scalawag.

Sharp GASP from crew.

CAPTAIN ROBERTS

Scalawag?! I’ll have you flogged, flayed and fed to the fishes!

BENJAMIN

Why not clapped, cudgeled, keelhauled and quartered? Or is that too bold for a bilious, barnacle-brained, blackguardly bastard such as yourself?

CAPTAIN ROBERTS

Belay that babbling and button your blathering beak, you blithering braggart, before I…

BENJAMIN

What?

CAPTAIN ROBERTS

Before…

For the first time, he’s speechless.

BENJAMIN

…I’m bound, bled, boiled, and buried in the bilge?

Captain Roberts ROARS in frustration.

CAPTAIN ROBERTS

Enough! Prepare the Starfish!

A general cheer goes up.

The Starfish

The sounds of irons being clapped around Benjamin’s arms and legs.

BENJAMIN

Ow! Let me go!

(beat)

I said, let me go!

PIRATE 1

Come on, now, it’ll be easier for everyone if you just die quietly. Hand me that chain there, Hogwater.

Chain crashing on chain.

PIRATE 2

I said “hand,” not “throw.”

BENJAMIN

What’s “the Starfish”?

PIRATE 2

Oh, you’ll see.

PIRATE 1

(helpfully, almost fondly)

Basically, we lash your hands and feet to the four winches — one for each. And then we wind ‘em up, you know. Wind and wind…

PIRATE 2

Ooooh! And stretch.

PIRATE 1

Of course stretch — that’s implicit, you dolt. Anyway, all that stuff until you sort of… come apart.

PIRATE 2

Explode, like.

PIRATE 1

Will you please — !

(a beat)

Oh, all right. Explode. Anyway, I think you’ll quite enjoy it.

BENJAMIN

Okay, well…

(thinking, thinking)

…whatever you do, just don’t make me walk the plank.

PIRATE 1

Who said anything about the plank?

BENJAMIN

I just know it’s a thing pirates do and I’m really, really scared of that.

PIRATE 2

Oh, when we hook you into the starfish, you’ll be scared, alright…

BENJAMIN

I don’t think so, actually. I’ve never seen anything like it, so I won’t be able to imagine it. But I almost drowned once, so I can really easily imagine…

…PIRATE 2 …the plank.

PIRATE 1

Should we tell the Cap’n?

BENJAMIN

No, don’t! He’ll want to make me walk the plank! Please!

PIRATE 2

Indubitably we should.

Walking the Plank

EXT. THE NAGLFAR, THE PLANK - CONTINUOUS

CAPTAIN ROBERTS

I’m glad we could accommodate your preference for means of bodily destruction. So ye know, it isn’t the drownin’ that kills ye. It’s the creatures below.

BENJAMIN

Creatures?

CAPTAIN ROBERTS

Oh, aye. Big ones with big teeth; small ones with little teeth; soft and seductive ones that grip you to death in a thousand sticky arms. And you’d better hope one of that lot’s the ones that find ye. For if not, then the demon’ll get ye.

PIRATE 1

Tell ’im about the…

CAPTAIN ROBERTS

Shut up!

(beat)

You’ll see lights from below – those are its eyes, like ghastly lighthouses – and then a great darkness will open up that will seem to fill the world and then it will swallow you and yer body will disappear but not your mind. Your mind will remain, floating in darkness, for all eternity.

(beat)

If you really are a sorcerer, o Benjamin, my boy, perhaps you’d like to use your magic now.

PIRATE 1

Go on, with ya.

BENJAMIN

I’m going. It’s hard to balance.

PIRATE 2

Hard to balance, he says! Walkin’ the plank!

CAPTAIN ROBERTS

Have ye any dying declarations, O damned soul we deliver to the deep?

BENJAMIN

You’re going to regret this.

Roberts LAUGHS, the crew LAUGHS.

CAPTAIN ROBERTS

In he goes!

BENJAMIN

Whoa!

SWISH! He’s in the air…

…and…

Hollis to the Rescue

CLUNK!

HOLLIS

Ow!

BENJAMIN

Hollis!

HOLLIS

Grab those oars and row!

BENJAMIN

Thank god, I was…

HOLLIS

I know. Shut up and row!

As the pirates SHOUT in anger, Roberts can be heard yelling orders:

CAPTAIN ROBERTS

Lower the boats! Lower ’em, damn your eyes — now! Man the dinghies! Six to an oar, move, move! Cut the lines, damn you! Cut ’em!

BENJAMIN

Thanks for coming after me…

HOLLIS

We’re about to have about a hundred dead pirates on our tail, kid. Don’t thank me yet. Now STROKE! STROKE! STROKE! STROKE!
